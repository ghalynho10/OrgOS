# Q20 in Cypher, clause by clause

Question: who are the top 5 people by total logged hours across multiple projects
between July 1 and September 30, 2009 (UTC), ranked from most to least?

Both files read the `LOGGED_TIME` relationship from a `Person` (`personKey`) to an
`Issue` (`historyId`, signed `seconds`, and `loggedAt`, the history's `created` stored
as a UTC instant in the `Z` form, spec 0008 AC-17), the `Issue` node (`issueKey`) and its
`IN_PROJECT` relationship to `Project` (`projectKey`).

## The rulings this pair follows

Your rulings of 2026-11-02, recorded in the body of main repo commit `25e339a`: ruling 2,
`project` is listed and holds the projects of the listed issues and entries; ruling 3,
`person` is listed, returned people only, and Q20's one step lists `issue`, `person`,
`project` and `time_entry`. The accepted plan: the scope is the seven projects, with the
other conditions inside it. The standing sources rule is in commits `2ecf29c` and
`b13f405`.

## `Q20.answer.cypher`

- `MATCH (x:Person)-[t:LOGGED_TIME]->(i:Issue)-[:IN_PROJECT]->(pr:Project) WHERE ...`:
  **step 1** and **step 2**. `pr.projectKey IN [...]` is the seven project population,
  each entry with its one issue's current project. `t.loggedAt >= datetime(
  '2009-07-01T00:00:00Z') AND t.loggedAt < datetime('2009-10-01T00:00:00Z')` is the half
  open interval in the `Z` form, never a named zone (checklist item 2). `t.seconds > 0`
  keeps positive entries (checklist item 7). The flagged bot set is empty (spec 0006
  AC-17).
- `WITH x, count(DISTINCT pr) AS projects, sum(t.seconds) AS seconds`: **step 3**, the
  distinct qualifying projects per author, and **step 4**, the author's qualifying
  amounts summed, each entry once since each relationship is one entry (checklist item
  5).
- `WHERE projects >= 2`: **step 3**, at least two distinct projects.
- `ORDER BY seconds DESC`, `collect`, `coalesce(ranked[4].seconds, ranked[-1].seconds)
  AS cutoff` and `WHERE r.seconds >= cutoff`: **step 5**. The list is sorted highest
  first; the cutoff is the fifth total (index 4), or the lowest when fewer than five
  qualify; keeping every total at or above it keeps the top five and every person tied
  with the fifth. The `4` is the fifth place of the question's own five (checklist item
  10).
- `RETURN ... ORDER BY seconds DESC, person_key`: rank order, with ties in key order.

The Q20 adapter turns each row, in the query's order, into `(person:<person_key>,
seconds)`. It adds and drops nothing.

## `Q20.sources.1.cypher`

The known answer has one source step, with kinds `issue`, `person`, `project` and
`time_entry` (ruling 3).

- The same match, conditions and cut as the answer (**step 1** to **step 5**), with each
  author's qualifying entries collected next to their total.
- `UNWIND r.entries AS e RETURN DISTINCT ...`: every qualifying entry of each returned
  person, the issue it is on, that issue's project and the person. Only the returned
  people's entries are listed (ruling 3), and the projects are those of the listed
  issues and entries (ruling 2). The columns are read as sets.

## Checklist notes

Item 3: the answer sums seconds, which the question asks for, and no group is empty.
Item 4: a person with fewer than two projects is outside the ranking by the note's own
rule, so no zero row is owed. Item 6 does not arise: a missing property is absent in the
graph and no key used here can be empty. No path is built (item 9). Numbers written: the
dates, the `2` of "multiple projects" and the `4` of the fifth place, all from the
question or reading note (item 10).

## The difference from the SQL pair

None in meaning. SQL bounds `logged_at` with a `+00` offset; the graph bounds `loggedAt`
in the `Z` form. SQL cuts with `rank() <= 5`; the graph cuts at the fifth total, the same
set.
