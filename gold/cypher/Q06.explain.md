# Q06 in Cypher, clause by clause

Question: between October 1 and December 31, 2009 (UTC), which five issues across all
projects had the most logged hours while remaining unresolved, ranked with their projects?

Both files read the read only `neo4j` database: `LOGGED_TIME` relationships from a
`Person` to an `Issue` (signed `seconds`, `loggedAt` in the `Z` form, `historyId`), and the
issue's one `IN_PROJECT` relationship to a `Project`.

## `Q06.answer.cypher`

- `MATCH (:Person)-[t:LOGGED_TIME]->(i:Issue)-[:IN_PROJECT]->(p:Project)`: one row per
  entry; each issue has one project, so no entry is repeated (checklist item 5).
- `p.projectKey IN [...] AND (i.resolutionName IS NULL OR i.resolutionName = '')`:
  **step 1**, the seven project population with a current resolution that is absent or
  exactly `''` (checklist item 6).
- `t.seconds > 0` and `t.loggedAt >= datetime('2009-10-01T00:00:00Z') AND t.loggedAt <
  datetime('2010-01-01T00:00:00Z')`: **step 2**, positive entries (checklist item 7)
  logged in the half open UTC interval, in the `Z` form (checklist item 2).
- `WITH i, p, sum(t.seconds) AS seconds`: **step 3** and **step 4**. An issue with no
  retained entry never reaches this row, so it is excluded; the retained amounts are
  summed per issue, in seconds.
- `ORDER BY seconds DESC` then `collect({...}) AS ranked`: **step 5**, the issues as one
  list, highest first.
- `coalesce(ranked[4].seconds, ranked[-1].seconds) AS cutoff`: the fifth total (index 4,
  since a list starts at 0), or the last and lowest total when fewer than five issues
  qualify (index 4 is then out of range and gives null). With no issue at all both are
  null and the `UNWIND` below yields nothing.
- `UNWIND ranked AS r ... WHERE r.seconds >= cutoff`: **step 5**, the tie inclusive cut:
  every issue whose total is at least the fifth total, so the top five and every issue
  tied with the fifth. The 4 and the 5 it stands for are the question's own number
  (checklist item 10).
- `RETURN r.issue_key, r.project_key, r.seconds ORDER BY seconds DESC, issue_key`: the rank
  order the scorer reads; tied issues in a repeatable order.

The Q06 adapter turns each row, in order, into `issue:<issue_key>` with its `seconds`. It
reads no `project_key` (a ranking item carries an ID and a value only) and cuts nothing.

## `Q06.sources.1.cypher`

The known answer has one source step, with kinds `issue` and `time_entry`.

- The same `MATCH` and `WHERE` (**step 1**, **step 2**), then `collect(t.historyId) AS
  entries` beside the sum (**step 3**, **step 4**), so each issue carries exactly the
  entries its total was summed from.
- The same sort, cutoff and cut (**step 5**), then `UNWIND r.entries`: one row per
  contributing entry of a returned issue, `issue` and `time_entry` (the bare history ID).
  The `issue` column repeats per entry and is read as a set; no cell is empty.

## The difference from the SQL pair

The same reading, with a different way to make the cut. SQL uses `rank() <= 5`; Cypher
keeps every total at or above the fifth total. Both keep the same rows: a total above the
fifth ranks before it, a total equal to it shares its rank of at most 5, and a total below
it ranks 6 or later.
